The Game Concept

Infinite Run & Jump Game

Slime Jump is an endless runner game where players control a creature called "slime" to avoid wooden block obstacles. The slime can jump to avoid these obstacles. In this game, the slime will only encounter straight obstacles in a side view perspective.

The Game Flow

In short, the game flow begins when the player presses the start button on the menu to begin the game. Then, the player starts playing, and the score keeps increasing over time until the player loses. When the player loses, they will enter the game over screen to choose the option to play again or exit the game.
